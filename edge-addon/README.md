# Prime Only (Edge add-on)

This greys out every tile on primevideo.com that you'd have to pay extra for. That covers rentals, purchases and channels you don't subscribe to. Titles included with Prime, free with ads, or on a channel you do subscribe to (Shudder, for now) stay as they are.

- **How it decides.** The tiles themselves don't say, so the add-on reads each title's own page once in the background. That page shows "Entitled" when you can watch it. Each answer is remembered for two days, so a channel that ends drops out within two days.
- **The pill at the bottom left** switches between **dim** (the default), **hide** and **off**, and counts the paid tiles. Hide can leave gaps in carousels.
- **It works on the computer only.** On the TV and phones, use the What to Watch app.

## Install (once)
1. In Edge, go to `edge://extensions`.
2. Turn on **Developer mode** (bottom left).
3. Click **Load unpacked** and pick this `edge-addon` folder.
4. Open primevideo.com. The tiles start dimming as you scroll.

To update it after a change, click **Reload** on its card in `edge://extensions`.
